# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Prospective clients and employers reviewing John Rey Amad’s work, plus stakeholders who need to review and download client-ready project artifacts.

## Product Purpose

Present John Rey Amad’s full-stack development work clearly and provide polished, downloadable supporting documents when needed.

## Operating Context

The main portfolio is viewed on desktop and mobile. The benefits comparison at `/case-study` is intended for on-screen review and landscape PDF or PNG export.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Existing portfolio content and contact workflow must remain functional.
- The benefits comparison must preserve every label, value, blank cell, and plan name shown in `public/case+study+1(2)(1).webp`; it must not invent or omit information.
- The comparison should avoid the appearance of an Excel spreadsheet.

## Evidence on Hand

- Portfolio project imagery in `public/`.
- Source benefits comparison in `public/case+study+1(2)(1).webp`.
- Confirmed education and professional information in `src/app/page.tsx`.

## Product Principles

- Let real work and concrete outcomes lead.
- Prefer clear hierarchy over decorative complexity.
- Preserve source data exactly in client-facing artifacts.
- Make important artifacts easy to review and export.
